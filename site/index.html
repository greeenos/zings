    Self-host

    One container.
    Your workspace.

    1. Run it

      App, database, auth and sync ship in a single 45 MB image that runs in about 30 MB of RAM.

    2. Create your account

      Open the app, sign up and invite whoever you plan with.

    3. Install everywhere

      Add it to your phone and desktop as a PWA.
